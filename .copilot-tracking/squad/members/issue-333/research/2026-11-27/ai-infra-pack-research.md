# Task Research: AI-ready infrastructure and AI landing zones (#333)

Date: 2026-11-27 (caller-supplied) · Researcher: Squad Researcher · Mode: convergence recommendation, not approval.

## Executive Summary

Use a new opt-in `ai-infra` pack with separate blueprint and platform-options references. It covers the shared Azure platform beneath AI workloads without activating Foundry application guidance or attaching AI policy to every Azure/AKS squad. Reuse the current cast and lifecycle. The two references should work even if the user declines every skill; live readiness claims still require target evidence. No new role, default-profile change, or release-time SKU/capacity/price table.

The strongest external opportunities are existing pinned rows, not a new wholesale supplier install. In particular, reuse the existing APIM design skill through a scoped second pack membership if its consumption can be isolated, rather than asking consumers to enable Foundry for a shared gateway. The current legacy Scribe cold set has **2 bytes** of headroom; `00-index.md` is not a suitable link location.

## What You May Not Know

* “Zero new roles” and `Adds (roles)` are compatible: a pack seeds existing roles only where absent. The runtime can map the same registered skill identity to two opted-in packs; the existing prose-only reach rule must still be updated coherently.
* Portal-visible or approved quota is neither deployable physical capacity nor a reservation. The blueprint should expose separate evidence gates for SKU support, quota headroom, actual allocation/placement and commercial commitment.
* “Read-only” roles can author their assigned design/report artifacts; they do not mutate Azure. An advisory handover is complete without a developer dispatch, a deploy receipt, or fictional as-built facts.

### F2 — Keep AI platform infrastructure independently selectable (Q2, Q4; C1–C3, C5)

The three tests are self-sufficiency, exclusivity and whether the set answers mode/concern vs *what it is built on*. This scope lacks the methodology spine, fits next to several packs, and answers a platform question. One request with shared design/council is one squad; separately owned product and enterprise-platform workstreams may be federation peers, not prerequisites.

Proposed `Adds (roles)`: `architect, azure-architect, iac-author, deployer, cost-manager, observability, azure-diagnose, asbuilt-author, security`. `researcher`, `lead`, `scribe`, and any implementation/review spine remain profile-owned. Merely adding roles is not dispatch authority; a selected advisory track need not dispatch IaC/deploy/as-built. No specialist “GPU”, `networking`, or `identity` cast row. The original `azure` profile and `azure-cloud` remain unchanged for non-adopters; no new model pins or `routing=` or `depth=` semantics.

Existing wording demonstrating the pattern: `foundry-platform-options.md:21` says “Use this reference to frame decisions, not to answer volatile product questions from memory.” Resolve GPU/accelerator (including whether a referenced family is GPU or CPU HPC), region/zone, offer/subscription/VM-family quota, physical capacity, topology and placement constraints, storage targets and pricing, feature/preview, tool versions, availability of drivers/operator/exporter, deployment type and AVM module/version at point of use. Store the source URL/CLI read, timestamp, tenant/subscription boundary without credentials, region/zone, service/family/type, version, confidence and missing verification; compare mutually compatible tuples, not isolated facts. Recheck at readiness decision, plan handoff, deployment and material change. With missing credentials/availability, report supplied evidence and exact unverified condition; no quota request, reservation, price guarantee or live probe implied. `https://aka.ms/avm` is the current module discovery entry, not proof that a named item has a module.
